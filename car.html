Got an EIR quote? Compare tenures

Same loan amount as above. A longer tenure lowers the monthly instalment, but you pay more interest in total.

The EIR is capped at 17% a year up to 5 years and 16% above 5 years for fixed-rate loans.

Interest is worked out on the reducing balance with equal monthly instalments, the method the Hire-Purchase (Amendment) Act 2026 uses for new agreements from 1 June 2026. The flat-rate maths (interest on the full loan for every year) is how older agreements were quoted. Example from Bank Negara Malaysia: RM100,000 over 9 years at 3% flat or 5.5% EIR is RM1,175.93 a month and RM27,000 of interest. Insurance or takaful, road tax, servicing, petrol and late charges are not included. Your lender's figures may differ slightly through rounding and fees. Education only, not financial advice.
