Where to keep it

Somewhere separate from your spending account, that you can reach within a day, and that’s protected (check for PIDM cover). Not in shares or crypto: the point is that it’s there when you need it.

Use it for real emergencies only: job loss, medical bills, urgent repairs. Then top it back up.
