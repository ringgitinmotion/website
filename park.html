The ASB lowest-balance catch

ASB and ASM pay on your lowest balance in each month. Take money out for even one day and that month only counts what was left.

Illustration only. Savings and fixed deposit interest is shown as simple yearly interest; ASB/ASM is shown as the yearly payout rate on money kept in for whole months. Bank deposits are protected by PIDM up to RM250,000 per depositor per member bank (Islamic and conventional separately). ASB and ASM are unit trusts: not PIDM-protected and not guaranteed. ASB is mainly for Bumiputera; ASM funds are open to all Malaysians when units are available. Rates change. Education only, not financial advice.
